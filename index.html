Frequently asked questions

What counts as a “piece” of trash?

Any item of litter such as a plastic bottle, can, wrapper, or cigarette butt.

Can I pick up more than one piece a day?

Absolutely! The more the better.

How do I submit my photos? Does each photo need to be a separate piece of trash?

Send your photos to the WhatsApp group. Each photo can have as many pieces as you like, but please separate them out so we can count the individual pieces and maintain an accurate tally.

How long does it take for my trash to be tallied here?

It can take up to 3 days for your photos to be analyized and tallied here.
